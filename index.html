02 — Process

        
From idea to production
in four honest steps.

      

      

        

          
Week 01

          
Discover

          
A short call and a written brief. We pin down the problem, users, constraints and what "done" looks like.

        

        

          
Week 1–22

          
Design

          
Flows and clickable prototypes you can test with real people before a line of production code.

        

        

          
Weekly3

          
Build

          
Short cycles with a working preview link every week. You see progress, not status reports.

        

        

          
Launch +4

          
Ship & support

          
Launch, monitoring and handover docs. Your code, your repos, your accounts — always.

04 — FAQ

        
Questions,
answered plainly.

      

      

        
How much does a project cost?
It depends on scope. After a short call we send a written, fixed quote — you'll know the price before any work begins.

        
Can you take over an existing codebase?
Yes. We start with a short paid audit, share what we find, and then agree a plan for fixes and new features.

        
Who owns the code and designs?
You do. Work lives in your repositories and accounts, and full rights transfer to you on payment.

        
Do you work with teams in other time zones?
Yes — we work remotely with clients in any time zone and keep a few hours of overlap for calls.

        
Will you sign an NDA?
Of course. Send yours over, or we can provide a standard mutual NDA.
