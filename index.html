(04) FAQ

Common questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short call we send a written fixed quote, so you know the price before work begins.

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a short paid audit, walk you through what we found, and agree a plan for fixes and new features.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. The work lives in your repositories and accounts, and full rights pass to you once invoices are paid.

Do you work with teams in other time zones?

Yes. We work remotely and keep a few hours of overlap each day for calls.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send us yours, or we can share a standard mutual NDA.
